Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Holiday is a private personal assistant used only by one household. It is not offered to the public.

What data Holiday accesses

With the account owner's permission, Holiday may read Google account data such as calendar events and email, only to help that household organise its own work and family life.

How data is stored

Data Holiday accesses is processed and stored on the household's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.
